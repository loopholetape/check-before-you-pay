How to read the columns. "New entries" are the entries made 2 to 14 days ago, which is the best view of what a new entry gets today, and "Fee they paid" is the typical fee those entries paid, which matters when it is far from the fee now. "Still in" and "Fee back so far" are counts out of those new entries. "Paid a day today" is what a typical one that is still in is paid, and "Days for the fee at that pay" divides the fee now by that pay, so it holds only for an entry that lasts that long and is replaced by words where most entries are removed first or are not paid today. The two "Month" columns are about the entries made 14 to 30 days ago, each of which has had two full weeks: how many have by now been paid at least their fee, and what the typical entry was paid minus its fee before any cost of running it. A small group there says little. "Slots taken" matters because a full contest removes an entry for every new one.

The same list as data is [contests.json](contests.json), which describes every field, and [llms.txt](llms.txt) explains every word.

## Packs

The measurements and the notes on this site are free, and they cannot tell you how an entry of your own would fare. For that we read a subnet's code closely and write a pack, which is an account of what the subnet really pays for, with the file named for every statement about scoring, and a practice judge that an agent runs on its operator's own machine before the fee is paid. One pack is for sale: Pocket (subnet 26, [c/26.md](c/26.md), $60). A pack is paid for in TAO and comes with every update for 30 days, no account is needed, and the section "A pack for this contest" on the subnet's page says what it holds, what it cannot tell you and what a buyer should know about us. How to pay is described in [pay.md](https://check.loopholetape.com/pay.md), and the packs on sale are listed as data at https://check.loopholetape.com/pay/packs. One more pack is written and not for sale yet: rec4ll (subnet 31, [c/31.md](c/31.md)). The practice judge of the pack for Pocket can be tried for free before anything is paid, with a scripted model and no key, and [pocket-trial/README.md](https://check.loopholetape.com/pocket-trial/README.md) says how.
