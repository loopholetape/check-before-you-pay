The fee is the registration cost that a new miner burns, an entry is one hotkey registered as a miner, what came back is the miner's emissions valued in dollars, an entry that is removed has been deregistered, and the days it takes for the fee to come back are the time to break even.

## What mining here takes, read from its code

An EveryFrame miner offers to serve video, image or audio jobs, and its cloud worker calls a provider and returns the media with a signed receipt. To set up, the miner needs a hotkey, the key a miner works with, registered on subnet 117, along with a funded Phala account with a workspace token and a funded provider account whose terms permit serving customers. The miner must also accept the Miner Terms. The worker runs on a Phala tdx.small instance with the gateway disabled, and the computer used to manage it needs no GPU.

The earnings display reports work reviewed by the coordinator, while an actual on-chain payment depends on later steps involving a validator, a participant that scores the miners’ work, and the chain.

Offering a larger discount reduces the value of a job while leaving the provider’s bill unchanged. Hosting continues while idle, storage can cost money after shutdown, and providers can charge for failed jobs. The hourly hosting ceiling limits the compute rate and sets no total spending limit. A job can pass to another eligible miner after certain provider rejections, but the original deadline still applies. Read from https://github.com/everyframe-studios/everyframe-miner at commit 1a68c4e on 2026-11-23.

## What this page cannot see

These figures count the entry fee and nothing else. Some contests charge again for every upload or task, and running an entry has its own costs in machines, model calls and time, and none of that is in here. What the contest asks of an entry is set out in the section above, which AI agents read from its code on 2026-11-23, and since code changes, the contest's own code and rules remain the authority. An entry that came and went within four hours can be missed, and a wallet is only an address, so one person can stand behind several.
