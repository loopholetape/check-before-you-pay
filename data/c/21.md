# AdTAO, Bittensor subnet 21: what happened to the entries that paid to get in

This page is written for an operator, or for the agent working for one, who is about to pay the entry fee of AdTAO. Everything on it was read from the Bittensor chain up to November 26, 2026 at 12:05 UTC, nothing is predicted, and every dollar amount uses the price of the day it was paid.

In Bittensor's own words this page is about mining on subnet 21. The fee is the registration cost that a new miner burns, an entry is one hotkey registered as a miner, what came back is the miner's emissions valued in dollars, an entry that is removed has been deregistered, and the days it takes for the fee to come back are the time to break even.

The contest describes itself as follows, and these are its own words copied from the chain, not a judgment of this site: "Counterfactual impact prediction for advertising interventions"

The fee is $18.26 right now, and over the last 30 days it has been as low as $18.06 and as high as $31.34, because it moves with demand. The fee is burned when the entry is made and is never returned, whether or not the entry is ever paid.

## What mining here takes, read from its code

As a miner on AdTAO, you predict how Google Ads changes affect spending, conversions and efficiency over 7, 14 and 28 days. You register a wallet hotkey, the key you work with as a miner, on mainnet subnet 21, bind an Ed25519 key and commit your model’s fingerprint before making the image public. The operator runs it daily, so you don’t need an always-on prediction server. The published local minimum includes two 2 GHz x86-64 CPU cores, 4 GB of RAM and 5 GB of SSD storage, and you don’t need a GPU.

To get in, you have to beat the baseline by more than 5% while covering at least 90% of its outcomes. The specification gives the cutoff as midnight EST, and forecasts settle 10, 17 and 31 days after the change. Scores reward accurate ranges and estimates of how much and in which direction the advertiser’s goal metric changes when it can be measured. Miners are ranked on the same cases over 28 days, with a seven-day half-life, and placement normally requires 250 weighted predictions.

Payment also normally requires seven distinct scored days and 1,000 SN21 alpha staked to the hotkey. Up to 20 eligible miners split the pool by rank. The starting weights are 50, 25 and 10, and each weight after that is 80% of the preceding weight. The weights are scaled to sum to 100%.

If the model stays unchanged, it stops running on day 41, with its first run that delivered predictions counting as day one. Read from https://github.com/ippcteam/SN21-adtao at commit 9560df9 on 2026-11-23.

## What this page cannot see

These figures count the entry fee and nothing else. Some contests charge again for every upload or task, and running an entry has its own costs in machines, model calls and time, and none of that is in here. What the contest asks of an entry is set out in the section above, which AI agents read from its code on 2026-11-23, and since code changes, the contest's own code and rules remain the authority. An entry that came and went within four hours can be missed, and a wallet is only an address, so one person can stand behind several.
