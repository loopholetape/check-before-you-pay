# CliqueAI, Bittensor subnet 83: what happened to the entries that paid to get in

This page is written for an operator, or for the agent working for one, who is about to pay the entry fee of CliqueAI. Everything on it was read from the Bittensor chain up to November 22, 2026 at 08:05 UTC, nothing is predicted, and every dollar amount uses the price of the day it was paid.

The contest describes itself as follows, and these are its own words copied from the chain, not a judgment of this site: "CliqueAI - AI-Powered Maximum Clique Solver Network"

## If you are about to pay the entry fee

The fee is $18.59 right now, and over the last 30 days it has been as low as $18.06 and as high as $86.44, because it moves with demand. The fee is burned when the entry is made and is never returned, whether or not the entry is ever paid.

Of the 24 entries made 2 to 14 days ago, 7 are still in the contest and none has its fee back. A typical one that is still in is paid nothing today, so at today's pay the fee does not come back.

## The contest today

All 256 slots are taken, so every new entry removes the lowest-paid entry that is past its protected period, which lasts about 20 hours after an entry is made. $3,479 a day is paid to entrants today, 6 wallets receive $5 a day or more, and the largest wallet takes 34% of the total.

## How the entries from two to four weeks ago ended up

31 entries from 23 wallets entered between Oct 24 and Nov 7, so each of them has had two full weeks, and they paid a typical fee of $25.26. After 7 days none of them were being paid $1 a day or more, and after 14 days none were. None of them has been paid as much as its fee, and the typical entry ended $25.25 behind its fee before any cost of running it. By week of entry, oldest first, the share that got the fee back was 0% and 0%.

## What this page cannot see

These figures count the entry fee and nothing else. Some contests charge again for every upload or task, and running an entry has its own costs in machines, model calls and time, and none of that is in here. This page also cannot say what the contest asks of an entry, which hardware it needs or how it decides who is paid, and for that the contest's own code and rules have to be read. An entry that came and went within four hours can be missed, and a wallet is only an address, so one person can stand behind several.
