# Numinous, Bittensor subnet 6: what happened to the entries that paid to get in

This page is written for an operator, or for the agent working for one, who is about to pay the entry fee of Numinous. Everything on it was read from the Bittensor chain up to November 27, 2026 at 12:05 UTC, nothing is predicted, and every dollar amount uses the price of the day it was paid.

In Bittensor's own words this page is about mining on subnet 6. The fee is the registration cost that a new miner burns, an entry is one hotkey registered as a miner, what came back is the miner's emissions valued in dollars, an entry that is removed has been deregistered, and the days it takes for the fee to come back are the time to break even.

The contest describes itself as follows, and these are its own words copied from the chain, not a judgment of this site: "Numinous is a forecasting protocol whose goal is to aggregate agents into superhuman LLM forecasters."

## What mining here takes, read from its code

Miners on Numinous, Bittensor subnet 6, use numi to seal and sign predictions about company relationships or actions by states and armed groups. Rounds open daily, and only the miner’s last submission for each question before the deadline counts.

The guide requires Python 3.11 or later and a registered wallet hotkey, which is the key a miner works with. It specifies no hardware minimum, uptime requirement, public port, third-party account or API key, and it gives no protection period for newcomers. Registration costs TAO, and the amount varies with network demand, while the guide states no additional submission fee.

The README counts hits, but the code adjusts for each statement’s baseline likelihood. A hit earns one minus that likelihood, and a miss loses the likelihood. Validators, who are participants that score the miners’ work, use a daily cutoff of 15:00 UTC and compute credits after a results pull that starts after that cutoff. They total each bundle’s points judged in the preceding 30 days and divide by the square root of its statement count, which includes pending statements and excludes voided ones. They then add the bundle credits and floor the miner’s total at zero. From 16:00 UTC, the top 25 positive scorers share 98% of reward weight by squared credits, and all positive scorers share 2% by inverse rank, subject to chain limits. If nobody has positive credits, the formula assigns all weight to the owner.

News reported after the deadline must confirm the development by the question’s end date. A relationship the graph already held at the deadline counts as a miss, but if the graph had recorded only rumours, a prediction that the act will happen can still count. The same news event earns each miner credit only once, even across questions. Read from https://github.com/numinouslabs/numinous at commit 012ccac on 2026-11-23.

## What this page cannot see

These figures count the entry fee and nothing else. Some contests charge again for every upload or task, and running an entry has its own costs in machines, model calls and time, and none of that is in here. What the contest asks of an entry is set out in the section above, which AI agents read from its code on 2026-11-23, and since code changes, the contest's own code and rules remain the authority. An entry that came and went within four hours can be missed, and a wallet is only an address, so one person can stand behind several.
