In Bittensor's own words this page is about mining on subnet 43. The fee is the registration cost that a new miner burns, an entry is one hotkey registered as a miner, what came back is the miner's emissions valued in dollars, an entry that is removed has been deregistered, and the days it takes for the fee to come back are the time to break even.

## What mining here takes, read from its code

A Graphite miner sends routes for one or more vehicles to validators, who are participants that score the miners' work, or proposes swaps to rebalance a portfolio. The listed minimum is four 2.5 GHz x86-64 cores, 8 GB of RAM, 4 GB of swap and 10 GB of free disk, with 100 Mbps in each direction. A CPU is sufficient, and the miner needs a reachable port and a registered Bittensor hotkey, which is the key a miner works with. The separate Taotrader guide requires an account and a balance of 5 to 15 TAO.

Validators compare valid routes with a benchmark and other replies, while portfolio scores give 60% weight to using fewer swaps and 40% to meeting allocation targets. When the published validator submits weights, it overrides those scores and gives UID 20 all its weight. The Taotrader guide describes hourly scoring, 300 blocks for adjustments and a 6,900-block trading restriction.

The guide describes a 60/20/20 split, but the code starts only the synthetic request loop, and synthetic batches start more than 25 blocks apart. Route replies get 30 seconds plus graph reconstruction time, while portfolio deadlines scale with the numbers of portfolios and constraints. The guides give no registration protection period or additional graph-mining fee. Read from https://github.com/GraphiteAI/Graphite-Subnet at commit 503b551 on 2026-11-23.

## What this page cannot see

These figures count the entry fee and nothing else. Some contests charge again for every upload or task, and running an entry has its own costs in machines, model calls and time, and none of that is in here. What the contest asks of an entry is set out in the section above, which AI agents read from its code on 2026-11-23, and since code changes, the contest's own code and rules remain the authority. An entry that came and went within four hours can be missed, and a wallet is only an address, so one person can stand behind several.
