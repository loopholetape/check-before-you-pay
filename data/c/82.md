# Noah, Bittensor subnet 82: what happened to the entries that paid to get in

This page is written for an operator, or for the agent working for one, who is about to pay the entry fee of Noah. Everything on it was read from the Bittensor chain up to November 23, 2026 at 00:05 UTC, nothing is predicted, and every dollar amount uses the price of the day it was paid.

## If you are about to pay the entry fee

The fee is $520 right now, and over the last 30 days it has been as low as $179 and as high as $3,753, because it moves with demand. Here 95% of that price is held as collateral on the entry and is released only as the entry earns, while the rest is burned when the entry is made, so an entry that is never paid loses all of it. This page counts the whole price as the fee and counts collateral as coming back once it is released.

Of the 8 entries made 2 to 14 days ago, all 8 are still in the contest and 3 already have their fee back. A typical one that is still in is paid $0.62 a day today, down from $27.18 a week ago, so at today's pay the fee would take about 843 days to come back, provided the entry lasts that long.

## The contest today

77 of the 82 slots are taken, so a new entry does not remove anyone yet, and an entry cannot be removed for about 9 days after it is made. $271 a day is paid to entrants today, 5 wallets receive $5 a day or more, and the largest wallet takes 45% of the total.

## What this page cannot see

These figures count the entry fee and nothing else. Some contests charge again for every upload or task, and running an entry has its own costs in machines, model calls and time, and none of that is in here. This page also cannot say what the contest asks of an entry, which hardware it needs or how it decides who is paid, and for that the contest's own code and rules have to be read. An entry that came and went within four hours can be missed, and a wallet is only an address, so one person can stand behind several.
