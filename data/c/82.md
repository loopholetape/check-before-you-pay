# Noah, Bittensor subnet 82: what happened to the entries that paid to get in

This page is written for an operator, or for the agent working for one, who is about to pay the entry fee of Noah. Everything on it was read from the Bittensor chain up to November 26, 2026 at 12:05 UTC, nothing is predicted, and every dollar amount uses the price of the day it was paid.

In Bittensor's own words this page is about mining on subnet 82. The fee is the registration cost that a new miner burns, an entry is one hotkey registered as a miner, what came back is the miner's emissions valued in dollars, an entry that is removed has been deregistered, and the days it takes for the fee to come back are the time to break even.

## If you are about to pay the entry fee

The fee is $396 right now, and over the last 30 days it has been as low as $179 and as high as $2,772, because it moves with demand. Here 95% of that price is held as collateral on the entry and is released only as the entry earns, while the rest is burned when the entry is made, so an entry that is never paid loses all of it. This page counts the whole price as the fee and counts collateral as coming back once it is released.

Of the 8 entries made 2 to 14 days ago, all 8 are still in the contest and 1 already has its fee back. A typical one that is still in is paid $0.56 a day today, down from $39.64 a week ago, so at today's pay the fee would take about 702 days to come back, provided the entry lasts that long.

## What mining here takes, read from its code

A Noah miner registers a hotkey, the key a miner works with, on Bittensor subnet 82 and transfers stake to the Ark, with a target of half the previous UTC day’s eligible spendable mining earnings. The miner signs with a local wallet through a public chain endpoint and pays the registration charge and transaction fees in TAO, but entry requires no upfront NOAH purchase, Ark deposit or always-on server.

The code ships with Legacy scoring, which rewards contributions and streaks of meeting the daily target. After the next completed UTC-day roster and a finalized epoch allocation, eligible newcomers can share up to 0.25% of the mining weight of the validator, the participant that scores the miners’ work. The lifetime allowance is about five spendable NOAH for each coldkey, the key that holds the funds, and for each hotkey, whichever limit is reached first. Ordinary miners divide the rest according to their weights.

The V2 candidate instead starts eligible miners at 0.2 points and assigns the validator’s mining weight in proportion to final points. If all three assigned daily reports are timely and verified, they could add up to 20 points in total, while an optional burn could absorb about 98% of miner weight after bonuses. Both features ship switched off.

Deposits are permanent, but they earn credit only when they identify the miner and arrive delegated to the Ark Validator. The reviewed repository material gives no confirmation of V2 activation or current payments and states no duration for post-registration protection. Read from https://github.com/Vora-Inc/NOAH at commit cb7fb57 on 2026-11-23.

## What this page cannot see

These figures count the entry fee and nothing else. Some contests charge again for every upload or task, and running an entry has its own costs in machines, model calls and time, and none of that is in here. What the contest asks of an entry is set out in the section above, which AI agents read from its code on 2026-11-23, and since code changes, the contest's own code and rules remain the authority. An entry that came and went within four hours can be missed, and a wallet is only an address, so one person can stand behind several.
