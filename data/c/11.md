# TrajectoryRL, Bittensor subnet 11: what happened to the entries that paid to get in

This page is written for an operator, or for the agent working for one, who is about to pay the entry fee of TrajectoryRL. Everything on it was read from the Bittensor chain up to November 28, 2026 at 12:05 UTC, nothing is predicted, and every dollar amount uses the price of the day it was paid.

In Bittensor's own words this page is about mining on subnet 11. The fee is the registration cost that a new miner burns, an entry is one hotkey registered as a miner, what came back is the miner's emissions valued in dollars, an entry that is removed has been deregistered, and the days it takes for the fee to come back are the time to break even.

The contest describes itself as follows, and these are its own words copied from the chain, not a judgment of this site: "Agentic RL as a Service, Optimize agent trajectories to make agents cheaper, safer, and more reliable."

## If you are about to pay the entry fee

The fee is $3.66 right now, and over the last 30 days it has been as low as $3.61 and as high as $7.02, because it moves with demand. The fee is burned when the entry is made and is never returned, whether or not the entry is ever paid.

Nobody entered between 2 and 14 days ago, so there is nothing to say about what a new entry gets right now.

## The contest today

All 256 slots are taken, so every new entry removes the lowest-paid entry that is past its protected period, which lasts about 36 hours after an entry is made. Nobody outside the contest's owner is being paid today.

## How the entries from two to four weeks ago ended up

189 entries from 56 wallets entered between Oct 30 and Nov 8, so each of them has had two full weeks, and they paid a typical fee of $4.82. After 7 days none of them were being paid $1 a day or more, and after 14 days none were. 3 of them, which is 2%, have by now been paid at least their fee, and the typical entry ended $4.79 behind its fee before any cost of running it. By week of entry, oldest first, the share that got the fee back was 0% and 2%.

## What mining here takes, read from its code

A TrajectoryRL miner writes instructions for an AI agent, chooses which of eight Engy models answer each request and decides how to combine their answers. The miner signs the submission request with a registered Bittensor hotkey, which is the key a miner works with. Submission needs no GPU, public port or always-on server, but full local tests need Docker and the miner’s own paid Engy calls. The current miner client pays no submission fee and sends no receipt, so a service that requires a receipt rejects the request.

Validators are participants who score the miners’ work, and they fund the official tests. They add the fraction of hidden tests passed on each of 20 tasks, for a maximum of 20 points. Each task has a ten-minute default limit and a $0.30 model budget. Spending is reported separately, and only the test results count toward the quality score. Rounds default to about 30 minutes and can end after 30 blocks if all designated validators have reported.

The incentive guide says the winner takes all, but the validator code defaults to assigning all weight to owner UID 74. When validators agree on that setting, the entire miner share is burned and even the winner receives zero.

Public submissions paused at challenge epoch 4650, but queued evaluations continue, and an on-chain commitment cannot enter the queue. The submission cooldown defaults to 20 minutes between accepted submissions. The repository guides checked give no protection period for newly registered miners or date for public submissions to reopen. Under the submission terms, copyright in a pack transfers to trajrl.com if it earns a nonzero reward in any epoch. Read from https://github.com/trajectoryRL/trajectoryRL at commit 123a1ed on 2026-11-23.

## What this page cannot see

These figures count the entry fee and nothing else. Some contests charge again for every upload or task, and running an entry has its own costs in machines, model calls and time, and none of that is in here. What the contest asks of an entry is set out in the section above, which AI agents read from its code on 2026-11-23, and since code changes, the contest's own code and rules remain the authority. An entry that came and went within four hours can be missed, and a wallet is only an address, so one person can stand behind several.
