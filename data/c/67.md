# Harnyx, Bittensor subnet 67: what happened to the entries that paid to get in

This page is written for an operator, or for the agent working for one, who is about to pay the entry fee of Harnyx. Everything on it was read from the Bittensor chain up to November 28, 2026 at 12:05 UTC, nothing is predicted, and every dollar amount uses the price of the day it was paid.

In Bittensor's own words this page is about mining on subnet 67. The fee is the registration cost that a new miner burns, an entry is one hotkey registered as a miner, what came back is the miner's emissions valued in dollars, an entry that is removed has been deregistered, and the days it takes for the fee to come back are the time to break even.

The contest describes itself as follows, and these are its own words copied from the chain, not a judgment of this site: "Deep research as a commodity. Faster, cheaper, traceable research — produced by a competitive swarm of miners on Bittensor SN67."

## If you are about to pay the entry fee

The fee is $0.10 right now, and over the last 30 days it has been as low as $0.09 and as high as $29.13, because it moves with demand. The fee is burned when the entry is made and is never returned, whether or not the entry is ever paid.

Of the 628 entries made 2 to 14 days ago, 193 are still in the contest and 529 already have their fee back. Those entries paid a typical fee of $1.93, which is far from the $0.10 it costs now, so their record says little about an entry made at today's price. A typical one that is still in is paid nothing today even so, which means that pay here comes in bursts or goes to very few entries at a time.

## The contest today

All 256 slots are taken, so every new entry removes the lowest-paid entry that is past its protected period, which lasts about 2 days after an entry is made. $1,267 a day is paid to entrants today, 21 wallets receive $5 a day or more, and the largest wallet takes 35% of the total.

## What mining here takes, read from its code

A Harnyx miner uploads a Python file that answers research questions, and validators, who are participants that score the miners’ work, run it on their own machines. To upload the file, you need a registered Bittensor hotkey, the key a miner works with. The script uses your chosen providers’ API keys, so your account can be charged when several validators answer the same question and when there are retries. The script-upload instructions give no minimum graphics card or disk size.

Each current source batch starts with 10 questions, and selected scripts answer 20 more. Tasks default to five minutes, or three and a half in fast mode, while batch execution defaults to 24 hours, with roughly six hours for evaluation. From source data version 12, one title rewards correct coverage without excess claims, while the Overall title combines 30% correctness with 70% judgment against reference answers on deep questions. The champions share up to 80% of miner rewards, with smaller payouts for repeated defenses, and eligible challengers divide the remainder by placement and originality. A failed batch with finalized tasks and entries pays participating hotkeys equally.

Uploads timestamped at the batch cutoff still count for that window. Harnyx’s public server instructions cover unreleased black-box mining, which initially pays zero. Read from https://github.com/harnyx/harnyx at commit 473e506 on 2026-11-23.

## What this page cannot see

These figures count the entry fee and nothing else. Some contests charge again for every upload or task, and running an entry has its own costs in machines, model calls and time, and none of that is in here. What the contest asks of an entry is set out in the section above, which AI agents read from its code on 2026-11-23, and since code changes, the contest's own code and rules remain the authority. An entry that came and went within four hours can be missed, and a wallet is only an address, so one person can stand behind several.
