# Axonry, Bittensor subnet 99: what happened to the entries that paid to get in

This page is written for an operator, or for the agent working for one, who is about to pay the entry fee of Axonry. Everything on it was read from the Bittensor chain up to November 27, 2026 at 12:05 UTC, nothing is predicted, and every dollar amount uses the price of the day it was paid.

In Bittensor's own words this page is about mining on subnet 99. The fee is the registration cost that a new miner burns, an entry is one hotkey registered as a miner, what came back is the miner's emissions valued in dollars, an entry that is removed has been deregistered, and the days it takes for the fee to come back are the time to break even.

The contest describes itself as follows, and these are its own words copied from the chain, not a judgment of this site: "New Paradigm of decentralized training"

## If you are about to pay the entry fee

The fee is $94.30 right now, and over the last 21 days it has been as low as $0.16 and as high as $178, because it moves with demand. The fee is burned when the entry is made and is never returned, whether or not the entry is ever paid.

Of the 165 entries made 2 to 14 days ago, all 165 are still in the contest and 5 already have their fee back. A typical one that is still in is paid nothing today even so, which means that pay here comes in bursts or goes to very few entries at a time.

## The contest today

All 256 slots are taken, so every new entry removes the lowest-paid entry that is past its protected period, which lasts about 17 hours after an entry is made. $7,506 a day is paid to entrants today, 1 wallet receives $5 a day or more, and the largest wallet takes 100% of the total.

## How the entries from two to four weeks ago ended up

## What mining here takes, read from its code

A miner on Axonry writes a method for an AI agent to improve a language model by changing its weights. Each registered hotkey, the key a miner works with, gets one entry, so the miner commits the method’s fingerprint on chain before publishing it through their GitHub account.

Axonry supplies four RTX 4090s for up to six hours. Before review, the miner needs $148 in available credit from subnet alpha, which must be deposited by the owning coldkey, the key that holds the funds. That covers a $20 review fee, up to $48 for GPU time and up to $80 for model use.

Axonry tests the edit against the champion, or initially the base model, on the same questions. The edit must hold its ground across six benchmarks and clearly improve at least two after accounting for noise. Validators, who are participants that score the miners’ work, apply the published weights, and only the registered champion earns the share left after any burn.

Under the v8 rules, the miner has 24 hours from the commitment to open the first matching pull request and 24 hours from the first correctable verdict to delete material while preserving the idea. The subnet owner’s hotkey is exempt from both deadlines and skips the review fee, which is otherwise kept even if the entry never runs. Deregistration lets an entry continue but suspends its rewards. The miner instructions and v8 rules do not state local hardware minimums, a public-port requirement, a separate miner API key, a registration protection period or a fixed interval between competition rounds. Read from https://github.com/AxOrganization/axonry at commit 668f412 on 2026-11-23.

## What this page cannot see

These figures count the entry fee and nothing else. Some contests charge again for every upload or task, and running an entry has its own costs in machines, model calls and time, and none of that is in here. What the contest asks of an entry is set out in the section above, which AI agents read from its code on 2026-11-23, and since code changes, the contest's own code and rules remain the authority. An entry that came and went within four hours can be missed, and a wallet is only an address, so one person can stand behind several. A new contest took over this subnet number on Nov 6, and nothing before that day is counted.
