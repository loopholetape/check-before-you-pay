This page is written for an operator, or for the agent working for one, who is about to pay the entry fee of Hippius. Everything on it was read from the Bittensor chain up to November 24, 2026 at 12:05 UTC, nothing is predicted, and every dollar amount uses the price of the day it was paid.

In Bittensor's own words this page is about mining on subnet 75. The fee is the registration cost that a new miner burns, an entry is one hotkey registered as a miner, what came back is the miner's emissions valued in dollars, an entry that is removed has been deregistered, and the days it takes for the fee to come back are the time to break even.

## What mining here takes, read from its code

A Hippius storage miner keeps pieces of users’ files online, serves them and answers challenges that prove it still holds them. Your subnet 75 hotkey, the key you work with as a miner, identifies your Hippius family account. The family must register on Hippius, which can charge a variable family registration fee, and each storage node needs a child account and a signature from its node key. With slot lockup enabled, the default is one lifetime deposit-free slot per family, and a paid slot takes a variable deposit that the family can reclaim about seven days after the child deregisters. With lockup off, no deposit is required.

The code combines recent reports across active family nodes, and data served counts for 70% while data stored counts for 30%, with diminishing returns on both. New families with a positive raw score get a minimum raw weight of 10 through scoring-bucket age 24, inclusive. The Bittensor weight calculation budgets 1% for storage miners by family weight and 99% for UID 238. Hippius separately pays storage emissions in hALPHA to eligible families by raw score, excluding the account associated with UID 238. Hippius rankings use six-hour eras, and Arion checks for storage payments every 14,400 blocks, about 24 hours.

Splitting the same bytes across more nodes leaves the base score unchanged. Downtime reduces the score, and the code averages penalties for strikes and integrity failures across contributing nodes. Deregistering a child forfeits that node’s unpaid storage accruals. Read from https://github.com/thenervelab/thebrain at commit 2eb6561 on 2026-11-23.

## What this page cannot see

These figures count the entry fee and nothing else. Some contests charge again for every upload or task, and running an entry has its own costs in machines, model calls and time, and none of that is in here. What the contest asks of an entry is set out in the section above, which AI agents read from its code on 2026-11-23, and since code changes, the contest's own code and rules remain the authority. An entry that came and went within four hours can be missed, and a wallet is only an address, so one person can stand behind several.
