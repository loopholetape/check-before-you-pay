# lium.io, Bittensor subnet 51: what happened to the entries that paid to get in

This page is written for an operator, or for the agent working for one, who is about to pay the entry fee of lium.io. Everything on it was read from the Bittensor chain up to November 25, 2026 at 12:05 UTC, nothing is predicted, and every dollar amount uses the price of the day it was paid.

In Bittensor's own words this page is about mining on subnet 51. The fee is the registration cost that a new miner burns, an entry is one hotkey registered as a miner, what came back is the miner's emissions valued in dollars, an entry that is removed has been deregistered, and the days it takes for the fee to come back are the time to break even.

The contest describes itself as follows, and these are its own words copied from the chain, not a judgment of this site: "Agent-first GPU cloud. Rent NVIDIA GPUs via web, CLI, SDK, or API. Providers earn 95% rental revenue + bonuses."

A typical one that is still in is paid $0.84 a day today, about the same as a week ago, when it was $0.91, so at today's pay the fee would take about 102 days to come back, provided the entry lasts that long.

## What mining here takes, read from its code

A miner rents out NVIDIA GPUs on lium.io and sets the hourly price. The Ubuntu host needs at least 8 GB of RAM, a public IP, service and SSH ports, and at least three reachable rental ports per split. If the verification image is absent, the installer checks for 140 GiB free on Docker’s filesystem, and if the image is present, it checks for 100 GiB.

The provider registers a hotkey, the key a miner works with, on subnet 51 and uses it to sign in to the Provider Portal. Subnet rewards also require a connected Discord account, and collateral is optional.

Every 15 minutes the validator, a participant that scores the miners’ work, checks the hardware, GPU, network, ports and Sysbox runtime. The code’s fallback rental-fee rate is 90%. Under the fallback configuration, qualifying rented GPUs share 7.5% of provider emission according to model, score and count, while eligible idle GPUs share up to 92.5%, with the unused portion of that allocation burned. The validator reads the ceiling from shared configuration, so the split can change. Emission pays about every 72 minutes.

Idle pay requires a price at or below the model’s soft limit and total disk capacity of at least 1.5 times total GPU memory. The checked passages give no post-registration protection period. Read from https://github.com/Datura-ai/lium-io at commit 8c5e49c on 2026-11-23.

## What this page cannot see

These figures count the entry fee and nothing else. Some contests charge again for every upload or task, and running an entry has its own costs in machines, model calls and time, and none of that is in here. What the contest asks of an entry is set out in the section above, which AI agents read from its code on 2026-11-23, and since code changes, the contest's own code and rules remain the authority. An entry that came and went within four hours can be missed, and a wallet is only an address, so one person can stand behind several.
