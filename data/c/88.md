The fee is the registration cost that a new miner burns, an entry is one hotkey registered as a miner, what came back is the miner's emissions valued in dollars, an entry that is removed has been deregistered, and the days it takes for the fee to come back are the time to break even.

The fee is $2.06 right now, and over the last 30 days it has been as low as $1.81 and as high as $3.07, because it moves with demand. The fee is burned when the entry is made and is never returned, whether or not the entry is ever paid.

Of the 75 entries made 2 to 14 days ago, all 75 are still in the contest and 22 already have their fee back. A typical one that is still in is paid nothing today even so, which means that pay here comes in bursts or goes to very few entries at a time.

## What mining here takes, read from its code

A miner on Investing chooses how to divide a portfolio among TAO and subnet tokens or among US stocks, or links a live or demo MetaTrader account through Know Your Miner so supported trades can be scored automatically.

Registration requires a hotkey, which is the key a miner works with, and each miner currently needs 192 Alpha staked and a separate 64 Alpha paid against the coldkey, which is the key that holds the funds. The optional local miner runs on Ubuntu 22.04 without a GPU and lists 20 GB of free storage, preferably 100 GB, and 1 Gbps in each direction. When allocations change, rebalancing also incurs trading costs in the portfolio.

Daily scoring runs at 00:00 UTC for TAO/Alpha, 02:00 for MetaTrader and 06:00 for stocks. Validators, who are participants that score the miners’ work, weigh returns against losses from previous peaks over a rolling 50-day window, and scores phase in over 30 days. The code excludes flat days from the win rate unless every day is flat, in which case the rate is zero, while the README formula counts all days. Rewards follow adjusted scores within each asset class’s changing share, so similar portfolios can earn less.

New miners appear after day one and have three days of immunity. US stock submissions must arrive before 09:28 Eastern for the open or 15:50 for the close, and the first submission fixes the portfolio’s asset class. Read from https://github.com/mobiusfund/investing at commit f04a153 on 2026-11-23.

## What this page cannot see

These figures count the entry fee and nothing else. Some contests charge again for every upload or task, and running an entry has its own costs in machines, model calls and time, and none of that is in here. What the contest asks of an entry is set out in the section above, which AI agents read from its code on 2026-11-23, and since code changes, the contest's own code and rules remain the authority. An entry that came and went within four hours can be missed, and a wallet is only an address, so one person can stand behind several.
