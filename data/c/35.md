# TTedu, Bittensor subnet 35: what happened to the entries that paid to get in

This page is written for an operator, or for the agent working for one, who is about to pay the entry fee of TTedu. Everything on it was read from the Bittensor chain up to November 28, 2026 at 12:05 UTC, nothing is predicted, and every dollar amount uses the price of the day it was paid.

In Bittensor's own words this page is about mining on subnet 35. The fee is the registration cost that a new miner burns, an entry is one hotkey registered as a miner, what came back is the miner's emissions valued in dollars, an entry that is removed has been deregistered, and the days it takes for the fee to come back are the time to break even.

The contest describes itself as follows, and these are its own words copied from the chain, not a judgment of this site: "The incentive layer for teaching. Tutor agencies compete on the live audience of their tutors' lectures on Telureo, and validators reward each agency by its share."

## If you are about to pay the entry fee

The fee is $18.32 right now, and over the last 16 days it has been as low as $0.09 and as high as $184, because it moves with demand. The fee is burned when the entry is made and is never returned, whether or not the entry is ever paid.

Of the 286 entries made 2 to 14 days ago, 251 are still in the contest and 1 already has its fee back. Those entries paid a typical fee of $0.13, which is far from the $18.32 it costs now, so their record says little about an entry made at today's price. A typical one that is still in is paid nothing today even so, which means that pay here comes in bursts or goes to very few entries at a time.

## The contest today

All 256 slots are taken, so every new entry removes the lowest-paid entry that is past its protected period, which lasts about 17 hours after an entry is made. $4,100 a day is paid to entrants today, 1 wallet receives $5 a day or more, and the largest wallet takes 100% of the total.

## How the entries from two to four weeks ago ended up

98 entries from 16 wallets entered between Nov 12 and Nov 14, so each of them has had two full weeks, and they paid a typical fee of $0.65. After 7 days none of them were being paid $1 a day or more, and after 14 days none were. None of them has been paid as much as its fee, and the typical entry ended $0.65 behind its fee before any cost of running it.

## What this page cannot see

These figures count the entry fee and nothing else. Some contests charge again for every upload or task, and running an entry has its own costs in machines, model calls and time, and none of that is in here. This page also cannot say what the contest asks of an entry, which hardware it needs or how it decides who is paid, and for that the contest's own code and rules have to be read. An entry that came and went within four hours can be missed, and a wallet is only an address, so one person can stand behind several. A new contest took over this subnet number on Nov 12, and nothing before that day is counted.
