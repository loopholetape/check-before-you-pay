# SayGM, Bittensor subnet 28: what happened to the entries that paid to get in

This page is written for an operator, or for the agent working for one, who is about to pay the entry fee of SayGM. Everything on it was read from the Bittensor chain up to November 24, 2026 at 12:05 UTC, nothing is predicted, and every dollar amount uses the price of the day it was paid.

In Bittensor's own words this page is about mining on subnet 28. The fee is the registration cost that a new miner burns, an entry is one hotkey registered as a miner, what came back is the miner's emissions valued in dollars, an entry that is removed has been deregistered, and the days it takes for the fee to come back are the time to break even.

The contest describes itself as follows, and these are its own words copied from the chain, not a judgment of this site: "Drop-in access to Claude, GPT, and Gemini."

## If you are about to pay the entry fee

The fee is $36.22 right now, and over the last 30 days it has been as low as $25.09 and as high as $67.28, because it moves with demand. The fee is burned when the entry is made and is never returned, whether or not the entry is ever paid.

Of the 39 entries made 2 to 14 days ago, all 39 are still in the contest and 35 already have their fee back. A typical one that is still in is paid $0.23 a day today, down from $11.17 a week ago, so at today's pay the fee would take about 156 days to come back, provided the entry lasts that long.

## The contest today

All 256 slots are taken, so every new entry removes the lowest-paid entry that is past its protected period, which lasts about 12 hours after an entry is made. $16,348 a day is paid to entrants today, 42 wallets receive $5 a day or more, and the largest wallet takes 81% of the total.

## What mining here takes, read from its code

A gm miner uses its own accounts to forward buyers’ AI requests to model providers and offers each model at a chosen discount. You need a hotkey, the key you work with as a miner, that is registered on subnet 28. You also need a Taostats login, a funded Phala Cloud account and API key, and a provider key whose terms permit this use.

You manage the worker from a workstation, and it runs on Phala with 1 vCPU and 2 GiB of RAM or, by default, with 2 vCPUs and 4 GiB. The default disk size is 40G, and port 8080 serves requests while 9902 serves authenticated metrics. A 10% discount sets your rate at 90% of retail, and you cover provider and hosting costs from that rate. Your miner earns emissions under its registered hotkey.

The current earnings command reads served value from the registry without btcli, so its dollar figure tells you neither your on-chain payout nor your profit. The miner setup and pricing materials checked give no fixed epoch length, registration protection period, first-service deadline or exact formula for each miner’s alpha share. Read from https://github.com/taostat/gm-miner at commit c2cd928 on 2026-11-23.

## What this page cannot see

These figures count the entry fee and nothing else. Some contests charge again for every upload or task, and running an entry has its own costs in machines, model calls and time, and none of that is in here. What the contest asks of an entry is set out in the section above, which AI agents read from its code on 2026-11-23, and since code changes, the contest's own code and rules remain the authority. An entry that came and went within four hours can be missed, and a wallet is only an address, so one person can stand behind several.
