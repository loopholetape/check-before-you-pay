In Bittensor's own words this page is about mining on subnet 91. The fee is the registration cost that a new miner burns, an entry is one hotkey registered as a miner, what came back is the miner's emissions valued in dollars, an entry that is removed has been deregistered, and the days it takes for the fee to come back are the time to break even.

## What mining here takes, read from its code

A Cascade miner writes a Python program that generates repeatable, 32-channel time series and pays to train a forecasting model on them. The operator generates the data on CPUs and rents GPUs against the miner’s provider key, so there’s no need to own a GPU. The configuration allows four or eight H100s or H200s, but the FAQ lists only eight-card rentals on mainnet and gives no live inventory. The November examples put an eight-H100 run at $60–95 and an eight-H200 run near $125.

Validators, the participants who score the miners’ work, compare forecasts with unseen observations, and a challenger has to beat the reigning model by a statistical margin. Across four equally weighted public benchmarks, the challenger’s geometric mean error ratio must be below one, and no benchmark can show a statistically significant regression beyond the 1% tolerance. The challenger’s task win rate must average at least 50% across the four benchmarks, and exact ties count as half a win.

The winner starts with 25%, 50%, 65% or 80% of Lane A’s pool and can reach 95% through later checks. Its predecessor can receive 5% for 36,000 blocks, and unpaid rewards burn. MarketCast is scheduled for block 9,579,600, around November 24 at 22:23 UTC, and once it’s active, its confirmed market-forecast winner can earn up to 25% of the competition pool.

Each registered hotkey, the key a miner works with, can have at most one Lane A entry judged per roughly three-hour round, and funding cannot guarantee a place. The guide gives no registration protection period. Read from https://github.com/TensorLink-AI/cascade at commit 40e2c34 on 2026-11-23.

## What this page cannot see

These figures count the entry fee and nothing else. Some contests charge again for every upload or task, and running an entry has its own costs in machines, model calls and time, and none of that is in here. What the contest asks of an entry is set out in the section above, which AI agents read from its code on 2026-11-23, and since code changes, the contest's own code and rules remain the authority. An entry that came and went within four hours can be missed, and a wallet is only an address, so one person can stand behind several.
