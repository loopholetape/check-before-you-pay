# Ralph, Bittensor subnet 40: what happened to the entries that paid to get in

This page is written for an operator, or for the agent working for one, who is about to pay the entry fee of Ralph. Everything on it was read from the Bittensor chain up to November 22, 2026 at 08:05 UTC, nothing is predicted, and every dollar amount uses the price of the day it was paid.

The contest describes itself as follows, and these are its own words copied from the chain, not a judgment of this site: "The same model, small enough to run on your phone. Compress the frontier models into GGUFs."

## If you are about to pay the entry fee

The fee is $184 right now, and over the last 30 days it has been as low as $179 and as high as $255, because it moves with demand. The fee is burned when the entry is made and is never returned, whether or not the entry is ever paid.

Nobody entered between 2 and 14 days ago, so there is nothing to say about what a new entry gets right now.

## The contest today

All 256 slots are taken, so every new entry removes the lowest-paid entry that is past its protected period, which lasts about 17 hours after an entry is made. Nobody outside the contest's owner is being paid today.

## How the entries from two to four weeks ago ended up

Nobody paid to enter in the last 30 days, so there is no record to report.

## What this page cannot see

These figures count the entry fee and nothing else. Some contests charge again for every upload or task, and running an entry has its own costs in machines, model calls and time, and none of that is in here. This page also cannot say what the contest asks of an entry, which hardware it needs or how it decides who is paid, and for that the contest's own code and rules have to be read. An entry that came and went within four hours can be missed, and a wallet is only an address, so one person can stand behind several.
