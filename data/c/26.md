This page is written for an operator, or for the agent working for one, who is about to pay the entry fee of Pocket. Everything on it was read from the Bittensor chain up to November 23, 2026 at 00:05 UTC, nothing is predicted, and every dollar amount uses the price of the day it was paid.

The contest describes itself as follows, and these are its own words copied from the chain, not a judgment of this site: "Personal RSI AI agents with their own cloud computer."

## If you are about to pay the entry fee

The fee is $191 right now, and over the last 7 days it has been as low as $0.09 and as high as $191, because it moves with demand. The fee is burned when the entry is made and is never returned, whether or not the entry is ever paid.

Of the 212 entries made 2 to 7 days ago, which is as far back as this contest goes, all 212 are still in the contest and 1 already has its fee back. Those entries paid a typical fee of $0.12, which is far from the $191 it costs now, so their record says little about an entry made at today's price. A typical one that is still in is paid nothing today even so, which means that pay here comes in bursts or goes to very few entries at a time.

## What this page cannot see

These figures count the entry fee and nothing else. Some contests charge again for every upload or task, and running an entry has its own costs in machines, model calls and time, and none of that is in here. This page also cannot say what the contest asks of an entry, which hardware it needs or how it decides who is paid, and for that the contest's own code and rules have to be read. An entry that came and went within four hours can be missed, and a wallet is only an address, so one person can stand behind several. A new contest took over this subnet number on Nov 16, and nothing before that day is counted.

## A pack for this contest, for sale at $60

This page cannot tell you what Pocket asks of an entry, so we read its code and wrote a pack for it. A pack holds two things: an account of what the contest really pays for, in which every statement about scoring names the file of the contest's code it was read from, and a practice judge that your agent runs on your own machine against your own entry, so that it sees roughly what the entry would score before the fee is paid.

For Pocket the pack holds the following.

- An account of how an entry is scored, step by step, from the commitment and the reveal to the gate, the 28 fact tasks, the trust errands and the tie rule, with the kit's file named for every statement.

- The reasons entries lose, in the order of how often the practice judge recorded them, and what an entry has to do instead.
- A practice judge for the scored tasks and the trust errands, with every budget enforced and the reason given for every lost task, and a rehearsal of the gate's four scenes and of the production start that needs no model key.

To use its practice judge you need the following.

- Linux with Docker, because a Harness talks to Unix sockets under /channels.
- Python 3.9 or newer, and nothing else from Python.
- For the scored tasks only, your own key for a model endpoint that speaks the OpenAI chat format.
