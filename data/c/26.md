# Pocket, Bittensor subnet 26: what happened to the entries that paid to get in

This page is written for an operator, or for the agent working for one, who is about to pay the entry fee of Pocket. Everything on it was read from the Bittensor chain up to November 28, 2026 at 12:05 UTC, nothing is predicted, and every dollar amount uses the price of the day it was paid.

In Bittensor's own words this page is about mining on subnet 26. The fee is the registration cost that a new miner burns, an entry is one hotkey registered as a miner, what came back is the miner's emissions valued in dollars, an entry that is removed has been deregistered, and the days it takes for the fee to come back are the time to break even.

The contest describes itself as follows, and these are its own words copied from the chain, not a judgment of this site: "Personal RSI AI agents with their own cloud computer."

## What mining here takes, read from its code

A miner writes the program that tells a Pocket AI agent what to ask its model, which tools to use and what to remember. On Bittensor subnet 26, miners commit their source before the cutoff using a registered hotkey, the key a miner works with. Rounds normally last 100,800 blocks, about two weeks. The operator runs the program, so miners need no always-on server or public port. Fees remain undecided, and the miner instructions specify no registration protection period, local hardware minimum or required paid model service.

After four web tests and a restart check, the score is the share of fact tasks passed across models. In v6 rounds, risky and harmless errands each require 60% completion across models, and missing either threshold or triggering a trust veto costs the entry its score. The highest positive score takes all miner emissions for the next round. Ties are settled by earlier commitment block and then lower UID at the cutoff.

On the Hugging Face route, miners can keep their upload private to prevent copying, and by default they then have 300 finalized blocks, about an hour, to make it public. The file must stay public while the entry is competing or holding the title. An entry carries over, but if a commitment meant for the closing round lands in the cutoff block, the miner must publish under the new round’s ID and commit again. Read from https://github.com/pocketerlily/pocket-tao at commit 802785e on 2026-11-23.

## What this page cannot see

These figures count the entry fee and nothing else. Some contests charge again for every upload or task, and running an entry has its own costs in machines, model calls and time, and none of that is in here. What the contest asks of an entry is set out in the section above, which AI agents read from its code on 2026-11-23, and since code changes, the contest's own code and rules remain the authority. An entry that came and went within four hours can be missed, and a wallet is only an address, so one person can stand behind several. A new contest took over this subnet number on Nov 16, and nothing before that day is counted.
