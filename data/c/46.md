# Instant, Bittensor subnet 46: what happened to the entries that paid to get in

This page is written for an operator, or for the agent working for one, who is about to pay the entry fee of Instant. Everything on it was read from the Bittensor chain up to November 23, 2026 at 12:05 UTC, nothing is predicted, and every dollar amount uses the price of the day it was paid.

In Bittensor's own words this page is about mining on subnet 46. The fee is the registration cost that a new miner burns, an entry is one hotkey registered as a miner, what came back is the miner's emissions valued in dollars, an entry that is removed has been deregistered, and the days it takes for the fee to come back are the time to break even.

## If you are about to pay the entry fee

The fee is $19.08 right now, and over the last 30 days it has been as low as $18.06 and as high as $25.72, because it moves with demand. The fee is burned when the entry is made and is never returned, whether or not the entry is ever paid.

Only 1 entry was made between 2 and 14 days ago, which is too few to say what a new entry gets right now.

## The contest today

All 256 slots are taken, so every new entry removes the lowest-paid entry that is past its protected period, which lasts about 36 hours after an entry is made. $1,138 a day is paid to entrants today, 6 wallets receive $5 a day or more, and the largest wallet takes 31% of the total.

A group of 9 is small, so these shares can swing a long way on a few entries.

## What mining here takes, read from its code

Instant is Bittensor subnet 46. The README’s hardware figures apply to validators, the participants who score the miners’ work. The validator README gives no miner hardware, uptime, or public-port requirements, and it specifies no third-party accounts, API keys, paid services, or extra fees for miners.

The validator records a score for each miner, and the scoring records include measures of speed, tokens, requests and success. The validator adjusts the burn against a dollar target for the miners as a group and divides the remaining weight in proportion to their normalized weights.

A successful request can have an unavailable proof, and a miner loses its score for the round if at least two proofs fail or time out and together exceed 0.5% of the proofs judged. The validator README gives no protection period after registration or deadline for a miner to answer a request. Read from https://github.com/Subnet46/sn46-validator at commit b3f0ed3 on 2026-11-23.

## What this page cannot see

These figures count the entry fee and nothing else. Some contests charge again for every upload or task, and running an entry has its own costs in machines, model calls and time, and none of that is in here. What the contest asks of an entry is set out in the section above, which AI agents read from its code on 2026-11-23, and since code changes, the contest's own code and rules remain the authority. An entry that came and went within four hours can be missed, and a wallet is only an address, so one person can stand behind several.
