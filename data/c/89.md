This page is written for an operator, or for the agent working for one, who is about to pay the entry fee of InfiniteQuant. Everything on it was read from the Bittensor chain up to November 27, 2026 at 12:05 UTC, nothing is predicted, and every dollar amount uses the price of the day it was paid.

In Bittensor's own words this page is about mining on subnet 89. The fee is the registration cost that a new miner burns, an entry is one hotkey registered as a miner, what came back is the miner's emissions valued in dollars, an entry that is removed has been deregistered, and the days it takes for the fee to come back are the time to break even.

The contest describes itself as follows, and these are its own words copied from the chain, not a judgment of this site: "Proof of Edge - Trading signals"

## If you are about to pay the entry fee

The fee is $4.71 right now, and over the last 30 days it has been as low as $4.52 and as high as $8.62, because it moves with demand. The fee is burned when the entry is made and is never returned, whether or not the entry is ever paid.

## What mining here takes, read from its code

On InfiniteQuant, miners submit encrypted calls on whether a market will rise or fall, or they enter an algorithm that manages a portfolio. IQ hosts signal miners, while self-hosters can submit individual calls with a relay token and a registered wallet hotkey, the key a miner works with, without a public port or paid price feed. A hotkey can submit three low-frequency calls per UTC day, spaced an hour apart with a 30-second tolerance for block timing.

Despite the README’s collateral statement, the code requires 500 SN89 alpha per separately registered signal slot to earn. The 40,000-alpha agent bond is checked against the entity receiving the rewards at the weekly snapshot.

A call is decided by whichever of the target or stop registers two price ticks first, and qualification takes at least eight decisive calls and statistical evidence of better-than-even accuracy. Signal miners share 30% of rewards in proportion to score, with up to 20 qualified wins fading over 30 days, while referrers receive 20% and agent books receive 50%. For the week starting November 23, half the agent pool rewards weekly gains above a previous high, and the other half rewards returns over up to 90 days, adjusted for drawdowns and trading history. Any long-term share left unearned because of short trading histories goes to the weekly pool.

The code describes 5,000 blocks of registration protection, while the eight-day scoring warmup begins at the hotkey’s first observed commitment. Read from https://github.com/DeltaCompute24/InfiniteQuant-Subnet at commit 05ce1f4 on 2026-11-23.

## What this page cannot see

These figures count the entry fee and nothing else. Some contests charge again for every upload or task, and running an entry has its own costs in machines, model calls and time, and none of that is in here. What the contest asks of an entry is set out in the section above, which AI agents read from its code on 2026-11-23, and since code changes, the contest's own code and rules remain the authority. An entry that came and went within four hours can be missed, and a wallet is only an address, so one person can stand behind several.
