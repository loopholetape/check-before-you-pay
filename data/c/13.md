# Data Universe, Bittensor subnet 13: what happened to the entries that paid to get in

This page is written for an operator, or for the agent working for one, who is about to pay the entry fee of Data Universe. Everything on it was read from the Bittensor chain up to November 27, 2026 at 12:05 UTC, nothing is predicted, and every dollar amount uses the price of the day it was paid.

In Bittensor's own words this page is about mining on subnet 13. The fee is the registration cost that a new miner burns, an entry is one hotkey registered as a miner, what came back is the miner's emissions valued in dollars, an entry that is removed has been deregistered, and the days it takes for the fee to come back are the time to break even.

## What mining here takes, read from its code

A miner on Data Universe, Bittensor subnet 13, can collect posts from X and Reddit, store them locally and serve them to validators, who are participants that score the miners' work. The miner can also upload batches to shared storage and fill requests for specific posts. The README also mentions YouTube, but the current miner code handles X and Reddit.

A CPU machine needs disk space, bandwidth and a port validators can reach, and the database targets 250 GB by default, plus overhead. You need a registered wallet hotkey, the key a miner works with. The default Reddit scraper needs no key, and Apify charges can apply if you use its token.

Validators favor fresh, requested data that few miners hold, and a validator can check a miner again after 60 minutes. For sampled on-demand submissions, validators check items against the request and fetch one item again from the source. When a sample passes, the reward depends on response speed and the number of items returned. Participation also affects the score. With zero on-demand score, neither local data nor bulk uploads earn weight. Before chain processing, the validator normally assigns 70% of its weight to the subnet owner and divides 30% among other miners by score.

Blocked requests from the default Reddit scraper can leave a responsive miner collecting nothing while its non-refundable registration fee is at risk. Local data buckets older than 30 days earn no credit, and the miner skips jobs with five seconds or less left. The guide mentions registration immunity without giving its duration. Read from https://github.com/macrocosm-os/data-universe at commit 42d8e3e on 2026-11-23.

## What this page cannot see

These figures count the entry fee and nothing else. Some contests charge again for every upload or task, and running an entry has its own costs in machines, model calls and time, and none of that is in here. What the contest asks of an entry is set out in the section above, which AI agents read from its code on 2026-11-23, and since code changes, the contest's own code and rules remain the authority. An entry that came and went within four hours can be missed, and a wallet is only an address, so one person can stand behind several.
