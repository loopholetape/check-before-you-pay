# Data Universe, Bittensor subnet 13: what happened to the entries that paid to get in

This page is written for an operator, or for the agent working for one, who is about to pay the entry fee of Data Universe. Everything on it was read from the Bittensor chain up to November 23, 2026 at 00:05 UTC, nothing is predicted, and every dollar amount uses the price of the day it was paid.

The contest describes itself as follows, and these are its own words copied from the chain, not a judgment of this site: "Scraping the world's social media data"

## If you are about to pay the entry fee

The fee is $1.36 right now, and over the last 30 days it has been as low as $0.59 and as high as $16.43, because it moves with demand. The fee is burned when the entry is made and is never returned, whether or not the entry is ever paid.

Of the 712 entries made 2 to 14 days ago, 88 are still in the contest and 206 already have their fee back. Those entries paid a typical fee of $6.51, which is far from the $1.36 it costs now, so their record says little about an entry made at today's price. A typical one that is still in is paid $3.12 a day today, about the same as a week ago, when it was $3.04, but most entries here do not last: only 88 of those 712 are still in, and an entry that is removed keeps what it was paid and loses its fee, so the days it would take to earn the fee back at that pay mean little.

## The contest today

All 256 slots are taken, so every new entry removes the lowest-paid entry that is past its protected period, which lasts about 40 hours after an entry is made. $665 a day is paid to entrants today, 27 wallets receive $5 a day or more, and the largest wallet takes 8% of the total.

## How the entries from two to four weeks ago ended up

959 entries from 130 wallets entered between Oct 24 and Nov 8, so each of them has had two full weeks, and they paid a typical fee of $8.04. After 7 days 130 of them were being paid $1 a day or more, and after 14 days 72 were. 224 of them, which is 23%, have by now been paid at least their fee, and the typical entry ended $3.37 behind its fee before any cost of running it. By week of entry, oldest first, the share that got the fee back was 33%, 27% and 17%, so it has been falling from week to week.

## What this page cannot see

These figures count the entry fee and nothing else. Some contests charge again for every upload or task, and running an entry has its own costs in machines, model calls and time, and none of that is in here. This page also cannot say what the contest asks of an entry, which hardware it needs or how it decides who is paid, and for that the contest's own code and rules have to be read. An entry that came and went within four hours can be missed, and a wallet is only an address, so one person can stand behind several.
