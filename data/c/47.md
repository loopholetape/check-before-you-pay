# Optia, Bittensor subnet 47: what happened to the entries that paid to get in

This page is written for an operator, or for the agent working for one, who is about to pay the entry fee of Optia. Everything on it was read from the Bittensor chain up to November 26, 2026 at 12:05 UTC, nothing is predicted, and every dollar amount uses the price of the day it was paid.

In Bittensor's own words this page is about mining on subnet 47. The fee is the registration cost that a new miner burns, an entry is one hotkey registered as a miner, what came back is the miner's emissions valued in dollars, an entry that is removed has been deregistered, and the days it takes for the fee to come back are the time to break even.

## If you are about to pay the entry fee

The fee is $18.26 right now, and over the last 12 days it has been as low as $0.09 and as high as $33.26, because it moves with demand. The fee is burned when the entry is made and is never returned, whether or not the entry is ever paid.

Of the 215 entries made 2 to 12 days ago, which is as far back as this contest goes, all 215 are still in the contest and none has its fee back. Those entries paid a typical fee of $0.15, which is far from the $18.26 it costs now, so their record says little about an entry made at today's price. A typical one that is still in is paid nothing today, so at today's pay the fee does not come back.

## The contest today

217 of the 256 slots are taken, so a new entry does not remove anyone yet, and an entry cannot be removed for about 17 hours after it is made. Nobody outside the contest's owner is being paid today.

## What this page cannot see

These figures count the entry fee and nothing else. Some contests charge again for every upload or task, and running an entry has its own costs in machines, model calls and time, and none of that is in here. This page also cannot say what the contest asks of an entry, which hardware it needs or how it decides who is paid, and for that the contest's own code and rules have to be read. An entry that came and went within four hours can be missed, and a wallet is only an address, so one person can stand behind several. A new contest took over this subnet number on Nov 14, and nothing before that day is counted.
